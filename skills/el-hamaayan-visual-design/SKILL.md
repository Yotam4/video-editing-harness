# El Hamaayan Visual Design

Make the viewer understand what matters first. Use [layout decisions](references/layout-decisions.md), [image-model briefs](references/image-model-briefs.md), and [evidence](references/design-evidence.md). Read `el-hamaayan-social-templates` to select an actual reference family before inventing a look. Use `el-hamaayan-engagement-lab` when choosing between creative approaches on performance grounds.

## 1. Set the visual job

Identify one dominant subject and one dominant message. Decide whether this is a founder story, place inspiration/information, product explanation, or Reel action. Choose the image before arranging the text.

- Founder/community: prioritize real people and the scene that makes their story tangible. Keep faces and meaningful gestures visible.
- Destination: make the place recognizable. Use a human for scale or lived experience when helpful; keep water, trail, view, or landmark visible.
- Product: prioritize the exact behavior in a real app screen, supported by a relevant travel situation. Enlarge a useful crop rather than shrinking an entire long screen.
- Reel: identify what changes in the first moments. Keep the opening action/reveal visible and make any essential text understandable without sound. Do not assume every Reel needs a title card or question.

Reject images that are merely attractive but do not support this post's promise. Do not generate substitute photos of real founders, destinations, or claimed community proof to fill a gap.

## 2. Build an intentional reading order

Plan subject → hook → proof/detail → action, adapting order to the story. Identify which element should dominate when viewed small; reduce competing badges, fonts, saturated accents, or boxes.

Use text in negative space or a controlled neutral contrast area. Avoid faces, hands doing something important, the destination's defining feature, and the relevant UI control. Do not mechanically put every title at the top or center: inspect the asset and placement.

Use size, weight, contrast, and spacing to create hierarchy. Use a limited accent color to emphasize the meaningful part of a headline, not every other word. Do not claim a particular color is scientifically best for our account or that an image model knows the optimal eye path.

Preserve the current family: the documented general brand system uses Assistant/Inter and Navy/Fountain; older approved founder/story references use Heebo and charcoal/orange/sand. Read the source family rather than silently mixing palettes or modernizing a locked asset. New work can adapt the reference when there is a clear reason.

## 3. Make text and proof readable

Use short natural Hebrew, deliberate line breaks, proper RTL, and a clear headline/body distinction. Keep mixed numbers, quotation marks, punctuation, and English readable. Preserve approved copy exactly; propose wording changes explicitly instead of silently cutting it to fit.

Render at the actual ratio and inspect at phone size. A screenshot that looks fine enlarged may communicate nothing in the feed. Crop to one behavior, preserve aspect ratio and authentic UI, and pair small context with larger proof when needed.

Prefer sufficient contrast over bright colors. Check text against the actual photo behind it, including bright and dark video frames. Use a restrained dark gradient, outline, or neutral copy region when the chosen family allows it. Preserve clean-footage rules in the approved one-shot Reel style.

## 4. Respect placement and source assets

Use 4:5 as the existing carousel master default (1080x1350) and 9:16 for Reel production (1080x1920), unless the owner or verified placement calls for another ratio. Make deliberate variants; do not mechanically crop a finished design across ratios.

Keep essential content away from edges, Reels controls, captions, and changing overlays. Use a current placement preview when available. Treat any numerical margin in the references as a starting design assumption, not a universal organic Instagram safe-zone specification.

Use original logos/wordmark/store badges as supplied assets. Preserve actual app screens as source layers for final production; image generation can alter fine UI text even when asked not to. Preserve identity, landscape facts, approved crop, and framing. Follow `el-hamaayan-reel` when its footage/crop protections apply.

## 5. Produce and inspect

For layout proposals, return the chosen image, reference family, headline/proof/action zones, type hierarchy, palette role, and crop rationale. When an image is requested, use the available image-generation workflow according to its instructions; follow the image-model brief reference. Do not generate deliverables merely to demonstrate that the skill exists.

Check both whole composition and phone-size output: focal point, readable type, RTL punctuation, faces, screenshot integrity, controls/overlay collisions, exact approved logo, and story payoff. Inspect Reel beginning/middle/end and the opening action. Revise one issue at a time and preserve approved elements.

Use a human visual review or actual eye tracking before saying where viewers looked. Call squint tests, grayscale checks, and model critiques editorial diagnostics, not measured attention or predicted engagement.

## Output

Return a concrete visual brief or exact visual fixes, with the source reference and any unresolved asset gap. Explain why consequential choices help this message. Keep measured performance in the engagement lab, sequencing in Self Attention, and rendering/export in the production workflow.
