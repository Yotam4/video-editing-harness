# El Hamaayan Social Templates

Choose the family that fits the message and reuse its design logic. Read [the reference registry](references/reference-registry.md) and inspect the relevant bundled preview before using a template. Read [adaptation and versioning](references/adaptation-and-versioning.md) before changing a master.

## Select a family

| Message | Family | What should dominate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who we are / why we built it | F1 founder story | Real friends, a concrete scene, personal progression. |
| How the work happened | F2 behind the scenes | Actual work photos, real evidence, the transition from idea to product. |
| Places / springs / trails / lookouts | F3 destination cards | The actual destination and one useful reason to go. |
| What this app feature lets you do | F4 product explanation | A benefit and one readable real screen/behavior. |
| Video moment / trip / natural reveal | F5 Reel action | The motion or scene; readable overlays only when needed. |

Use the user-named reference when supplied. Do not replace an approved family's fonts or palette merely to follow a generic new trend. Treat the general brand system and specific historical exports as distinct sources with scopes, not as one blended style.

## Workflow

1. Set the job and choose a family. Use `el-hamaayan-engagement-lab` for measured own-post examples; approval and visual quality alone do not establish performance.
2. Ground the actual Drive assets from the registry. Read file metadata before raw download and use the Drive workflow. Use bundled thumbnails for reference; production must use original-resolution assets.
3. Match the published creative before attaching performance. Verify exact image/text/order when possible. Keep caption/date/topic matches labeled as candidates until confirmed.
4. Select real photos/screens first. Use natural Hebrew and preserve approved copy. Apply `self-attention` to carousel slides 1 and 2 and the full sequence.
5. Apply `el-hamaayan-visual-design` to the focal point, text placement, contrast, and image-model brief. Use `el-hamaayan-reel` for actual Reel rendering and its approved one-shot mode.
6. Produce the required number of slides or shots; do not inherit a 10/12-slide count simply because the source had one. Adapt deliberate ratios and maintain source fidelity.
7. Review phone-size output, text/RTL, meaningful subjects, original logo, real UI, and both carousel entry paths. Version the result without overwriting the master.

## Source fidelity

Use real photos of us and our trips, plus authentic screenshots from the app. These are the owner's stated core materials. Use image generation for requested visual work with supplied references and clear preservation constraints; do not recreate founders' identities, geography, real UI, or documentary evidence as a shortcut.

Use original wordmark/Jeep assets and store badges, not generated approximations. Keep screenshot proportions, labels, and state accurate. Retrieve current product evidence before implying a screen still reflects the live app.

## Output

Name the chosen family, cite the actual Drive reference, explain what is preserved and adapted, and give the slide/shot plan or production result requested. State exact published-match status when citing results. Do not claim to have edited an unavailable original carousel skill; this skill is an independently usable template companion.
