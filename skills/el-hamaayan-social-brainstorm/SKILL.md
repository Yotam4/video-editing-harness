# El Hamaayan Social Brainstorm

Read the canonical harness at https://github.com/Yotam4/video-editing-harness using a checkout or connected GitHub tools. Load `docs/strategy/social-brief-and-brainstorm.md`, relevant brand knowledge and own-content evidence. Read `docs/research/brainstorming-methods.md` for provenance: our Maayan brainstorming fork, Superpowers and original design-method sources. This is a first-party social adaptation, not a software approval workflow.

## Size the task

For a small approved correction, preserve the brief and make the change. For a new clear piece, use a compact brief and reasonable assumptions. For an unclear concept or campaign, explore the uncertainty before choosing execution. Do not interview the user about facts already available in the record.

## Establish the creative job

Infer who should care, what they know, the useful/emotional/behavioral outcome, available proof/assets, constraints and approved words. Distinguish outcome from CTA. Enjoyment, beauty, friendship, recognition and storytelling are valid complete goals. Use audience stage as optional context; do not turn every idea into acquisition.

Ask only a consequential missing question, explaining which choice it changes. Continue independent work. Current user instructions govern the task; surface genuine source conflicts instead of silently following an older generic rule.

## Explore and converge

Generate two or three meaningfully different concepts when options help: vary premise, visible subject, proof mechanism, narrative, voice or production method rather than only synonyms. For each, give the promise/payoff, real assets needed, effort, main risk and evidence level. Recommend the best fit when the user has delegated the choice.

Keep recommendations tied to this item. Do not claim a framework guarantees performance. A real trip, an actual screen demo and a community-origin story are different approaches; three headlines on the same layout are not.

Use a slide map, rough storyboard or animatic to resolve the risky part before expensive production when useful. Do not impose an extra software-style design-approval gate on an already authorized reversible draft.

## Hand off

Use `el-hamaayan-content-router` and the registry to classify format, recipe, purpose, audience stage and assets independently. Record a proposed new-type candidate if the structure differs; do not automatically create a permanent skill.

Write the selected concept and rationale, brief, approved copy, proof, sources, constraints, classification, optional CTA, review checks and next step into the existing Git content record. Route to the relevant carousel, brand, natural Reel or product-video procedure. Preserve different voices when variants were requested.

Media and exports remain in Drive. Commit knowledge before refreshing its existing mirror under the Ela Maayan Hub. Never claim a save or publication that did not happen.
