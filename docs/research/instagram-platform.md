This is a knowledge document, not a hidden ranking recipe. The machine-readable [evidence ledger](social-evidence-ledger.json) holds claims, sources, access limitations and revisit triggers.

For practical Reel, carousel, and Story paths, use the [Instagram posting funnels](../strategy/instagram-posting-funnels.md).

## How to use this research

Start with the intended viewer experience and outcome. Choose a surface, content type, evidence and production approach. Then use the applicable skill. Platform facts constrain a plan; practitioner ideas and population studies generate hypotheses; our own matched examples help choose the next version. None supplies a guaranteed reach forecast.

Use five evidence labels:

| Label | Meaning | Example of permissible use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform fact | A scoped statement from Instagram/Meta, with date and source status | Eligible content can be recommended beyond its followers. |
| Observation | A result in a defined sample, including our own data | Carousels had higher engagement per reach in one provider sample. |
| Design principle | A general comprehension/accessibility principle | Put readable contrast behind essential text. |
| Practitioner recommendation | An experienced creator's or team's suggested method | Pair an opening promise with a real payoff. |
| Experiment | A proposed change whose effect is not established here | Compare a bus image with founders on slide 2. |

An owner's preference is binding creative input but not evidence of a ranking penalty. A field in a brief is not a reason to force a CTA. Humor, story, friendship and beauty may be complete outcomes.

## 1. Distribution is a set of systems

Meta describes multiple personalized ranking systems across Feed, Stories, Reels and other experiences, using combinations of predictions rather than a single decisive action. A share can indicate interest, but Meta explicitly describes multiple inputs and feedback. This supports a surface-aware strategy, not a universal like/share/watch-time formula. [S01](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/how-ai-ranks-content-on-facebook-and-instagram/)

The 2023 Instagram explanation distinguishes Feed's mixed home experience, Stories' connection context, Explore's discovery and Reels' entertainment/discovery context. Its original URL was rate-limited in this run; the original text was checked in a dated press-release republication. Use this as a historical surface model, not a 2026 signal-weight table. [S02 original](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained/) · [republication](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=305828)

| Surface | Useful viewer situation | Creative implication — our inference | What to compare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feed | Catching up with known accounts and encountering recommendations | Let a returning follower recognize us, while giving a stranger enough context | Reach mix, saves/reach, shares/reach, follows where exposed |
| Stories | A more immediate relationship with accounts a viewer follows | Updates, behind the scenes, replies, progress, participation and practical follow-through | Replies, available taps/exits, link or sticker actions; preserve provider definitions |
| Explore | Discovering something relevant without prior brand context | Make subject, value and genuine proof legible without knowing the founders | Non-follower discovery and subsequent profile/follow actions when available |
| Reels | Choosing the next video in an ongoing viewing experience | Show a reason to watch, orient quickly, sustain the idea and deliver its payoff | Available watch/skip signals, shares, follows; compare duration and age |
| Profile | Deciding whether an unfamiliar account is worth exploring | Clear covers, recognizable series and a coherent account promise | Profile visits/follows with attribution limits |

These are overlapping situations, not exclusive ownership rules. A product demo can introduce us to strangers; a scenic Reel can deepen affinity; a useful carousel can reach non-followers. Do not hard-code “Reel = top funnel” or “carousel = middle funnel.”

The publicly retrievable 2022 Feed card demonstrates candidate gathering, predictions, scoring and diversity rules. It is explicitly dated 2022 and must not be treated as today's complete Feed specification. Current Transparency Center cards were located but returned 429 in this run; their exact current predictions and dates remain a refresh task. [S03 historical card](https://ai.meta.com/tools/system-cards/instagram-feed-ranking/) · [current Feed](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-feed/) · [Feed recommendations](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-feed-recommendations/) · [Reels chaining](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/)

## 2. Eligibility, originality and audience fit precede optimization

Meta distinguishes content being allowed on a platform from content being eligible for recommendation. A distribution shortfall is therefore not automatically a creative failure. Read the relevant account/content notices and compare the intended audience before making an algorithm diagnosis. The 2020 announcement establishes that distinction; consult current policies and the publishing account when making a current eligibility decision. [S04](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/recommendation-guidelines/)

In its April 2024 original-creator announcement, Meta described recommendation changes favoring originals, replacing identical reposts and giving smaller creators opportunities. Its examples included original work, no visible watermark and conformity with recommendation rules. This is a dated announced model, not evidence that every eligible upload receives a particular test size or that its then-published thresholds remain current. [S05](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2024/04/ajudando-o-criador-de-conteudo-a-encontrar-novos-publicos/)

For Ela Maayan, original footage, founders, real screenshots, first-hand place knowledge and a distinct explanation are strong creative foundations. Reusing our own layout is different from reposting somebody else's media with cosmetic edits. This recommendation does not establish rights to borrowed material. Check the source and permission separately from recommendation eligibility.

Meta's January 2026 update reports increased original-content prevalence in **US** Instagram recommendations in Q4 2025. That reinforces the stated direction, not a numerical forecast for an Israeli account. A newer 2026 creator-originality page was discovered but could not be read directly; do not present its reported expansion to photos/carousels as newly verified policy in this run. [S06](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/) · [pending original](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)

## 3. Attention is an honest promise, proof and payoff

Buffer's original practitioner guide treats hooks as audio/visual/text openings that must be paid off by the content. That is a useful planning method, not a controlled experiment proving an exact opening duration, word count or hook category. [S07](https://buffer.com/resources/good-hooks/)

Our working application:

1. Name what the intended viewer recognizes: a situation, place, question, desire, useful contrast or human moment.
2. Make the opening legible through image and text together. Show the relevant action or real evidence rather than an unrelated scenic lure.
3. Deliver useful information or emotional progress early. A second setup slide needs to earn its place.
4. Make later beats add something new. Each change can answer a question, add proof, reveal a detail or advance a story.
5. End when the promise is fulfilled. Add a next action only when it serves the content and has a practical continuation.

“Shareable” is a viewer hypothesis, not a synonym for a sharing command. A friend might send a post because it solves a shared trip problem, expresses a recognizable friendship, starts a useful conversation, offers a surprising fact or is funny. Put the hypothesized recipient/reason in the brief, then observe the available metric. Do not convert all reported shares into DM sends.

## 4. Format choice depends on the job

Buffer's original 2026 report compares formats within a provider-published sample and distinguishes reach from engagement per reach. Its Instagram format evidence can inform a trial of Reels for discovery and carousels for richer interaction. It does not show every Reel/carousel behaves that way, isolate the format's causal effect, or predict Ela Maayan. Its article combines analyses with different windows; do not combine an older reach ratio and a newer engagement ratio into one 2026 benchmark. A companion landing page uses a follower-based engagement definition, reinforcing the need to read the actual denominator. [S10 report](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/) · [S11 companion](https://buffer.com/social-media-benchmarks/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026)

| Creative job | Good candidates | Proof needed | A useful response, if appropriate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition, enjoyment or affinity | Founder moment, humor, trip scene, brand story | Real moment, clear tone, coherent payoff | No CTA required; recognition or conversation may be enough |
| Practical planning | Place collection, route comparison, infographic | Verified access, scope, map/visuals and current conditions where relevant | Save, send to a trip partner or inspect the relevant destination |
| Product understanding | Real feature walkthrough, annotated carousel, short demo | Actual interface, accurate steps and a visible result | Try the demonstrated action |
| Confidence or objection resolution | FAQ, before/after workflow, genuine user example | Evidence that addresses the stated uncertainty | Ask a question or explore the relevant capability |
| Conversion or participation | A concrete event, contribution invitation or genuine offer | Correct terms, destination, eligibility and working continuation | One specific next action |

This is a choice aid, not a funnel requirement. A “commercial” can be introductory or conversion-oriented. A “brag video” needs a dated, defined proof point and a reason the audience should care, rather than invented scale or unsupported superiority.

Instagram's in-app Best Practices hub includes personalized creation, engagement, reach and guideline guidance. It is a useful account-specific check before imposing generic posting-frequency advice. [S12](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)

## 5. Music and editing tools are production choices

Meta's 2023 announcement confirms music support for photo carousels and describes collaboration features. It does not prove that a chosen track is cleared for this business, placement, region or future paid reuse. Its historical collaborator limit is not adopted as a current specification. [S13](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/music-and-collabs-on-instagram/)

Before using music, check the actual publishing account, format, intended organic/paid use and available rights/source. The licensed-music Help page and Music Guidelines redirected to login/blocked screens in this run. Keep those as verification destinations, not as documents claimed to have been read. Do not change account types to evade a restriction, promise that a song will remain available, or assume an original audio label proves ownership. [S14 Help](https://help.instagram.com/402084904469945/) · [S15 guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/legal/music_guidelines)

Meta's Edits announcement supports storyboard/planning tools, frame-accurate editing, exports without an added watermark, and feedback such as skip rate. Use these as possible production/measurement capabilities; availability must still be checked. The source does not show that editing in Edits grants a universal ranking advantage. [S16](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/introducing-edits-streamlined-video-creation-app/)

## 6. Comprehension and accessibility are part of creative quality

NN/g describes hierarchy through contrast, scale and grouping, and explains why text over photos needs enough contrast. Translate this into one clear focal point, readable proof and deliberate placement around the actual subject. It is a design principle, not evidence that a particular orange highlight increases Instagram reach. [S17 hierarchy](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/) · [S18 text over images](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-over-images/)

Use WCAG's 4.5:1 normal-text and 3:1 large-text contrast levels as an engineering/design benchmark, not a claim that this alone establishes legal or full platform accessibility. Inspect the whole region behind text and the actual small-phone rendering; a still frame does not validate changing video backgrounds. [S19](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)

W3C explains that captions convey meaningful speech and relevant non-speech audio for viewers who cannot hear it. For our videos, keep meaningful speech understandable with accurate captions or an equivalent supported text presentation, and review names, numbers, speaker changes and sound cues manually. Background music alone need not create a dialogue transcript. [S20](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/)

Meta has documented automatic caption and image-description features. Those announcements do not verify Hebrew support, accuracy or a particular 2026 account's interface. Add meaningful image descriptions through supported controls, and never assume automatic descriptions explain a detailed infographic or all on-image text. [S21 captions](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/05/recognizing-global-accessibility-awareness-day/) · [S22 photo descriptions](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/01/using-ai-to-improve-photo-descriptions-for-blind-and-visually-impaired-people/)

W3C's bidi guidance documents why mixed-direction strings can misplace punctuation and numbers. For Hebrew creative, preserve logical text, use the renderer's supported directional handling and inspect exported punctuation, product names and numerals visually. The HTML examples are not directly reusable in every image/video renderer. [S23](https://www.w3.org/International/articles/inline-bidi-markup/)

Preview covers, Feed, profile crops and Reels UI before export where available. Keep essential proof away from overlays. Use the actual current destination preview rather than a supposedly eternal safe-area pixel map; exact placement is a production check, not a ranking law.

## 7. Measurement separates the job from the scoreboard

Choose one primary outcome before comparing results. Keep its metric definition and denominator. Examples: non-follower reach for discovery when exposed; saves/reach for planning utility; replies for conversation; verified destination clicks or downstream events for a specific action. A delightful story may need qualitative audience response rather than an artificial install target.

Required context for a useful comparison:

- Publication ID, time, format/subtype, topic, intended outcome, primary change and source/creative version.
- Extraction date and post age; compare fixed-age snapshots when possible.
- Reach versus views/impressions, interaction components and exact exposed provider labels.
- Organic/paid, collab, repost and trial status; unknown is not zero.
- Available audience mix, watch/skip metrics and duration for videos; keep unavailable metrics blank.
- Matched baseline and alternative explanations such as topic, season, audience, distribution or a changed creative.

Do not sum post reach and call it unique monthly people. Do not compare a ten-minute-old post to a lifetime winner. Do not calculate unique sharers from share counts. Do not infer a funnel conversion from likes or treat average watch time divided by duration as a clean completion rate without matching definitions and replay treatment.

Trial Reels were introduced as a way to show a Reel to non-followers before sharing more widely. A later Mosseri article describes broader availability and reports observational creator results. Treat trials as a possible testing tool when available, not a randomized A/B test, a guaranteed lift or permission to change auto-sharing settings silently. [S24 launch](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/) · [S25 later update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/)

For the next experiment, vary one meaningful creative input, name the closest baseline, set a comparison window and state what would count as useful evidence. Keep production repeatable, but do not mistake repeatability for causal certainty. No schedule or publication is authorized merely because an experiment card exists.

## 8. Myths we do not encode as defaults

| Claim | Treatment in this harness |
| --- | --- |
| “There is one algorithm; optimize its secret weight.” | Unsupported universal model. Distinguish surfaces, eligibility and multiple predictions. |
| “Every post must convert or end with a CTA.” | Owner rejected this. Match the intended experience; no CTA is valid. |
| “All carousels are shown twice.” | Overstatement of a dated conditional claim; direct refresh remains pending. |
| “Music guarantees Reels distribution.” | No guarantee established. Check feature availability, fit and rights. |
| “Reels always beat carousels.” | Metric-, sample- and goal-dependent; compare matched own work. |
| “A weak post proves a shadowban.” | Diagnose eligibility notices, sample size, age and audience context first. |
| “Shares equal DM sends.” | Preserve the exact metric name; only calculate sends when exposed. |
| “Longer watch time proves better completion.” | Definitions, duration and replays can differ; inspect the actual measure. |
| “Questions, exact word counts, a posting hour or a color guarantee reach.” | At most hypotheses here. No verified universal rule. |
| “A public image is free to reuse; credit fixes originality.” | Ownership/permission and originality are separate checks. |
| “More advanced editing is always better.” | Use motion and effects when they explain, reveal or support the story. |
| “A familiar template is unoriginal.” | Format reuse can coexist with real original footage, proof and perspective. |

## Refresh and promotion policy

The ledger is a source inventory, not a list of equally verified truths. Records marked inaccessible or metadata-only cannot support newly asserted detailed mechanics. Recheck platform/eligibility/music/format claims before a production decision dependent on them, and after a meaningful notice, UI change or contradictory source. A 90-day review suggestion is our maintenance default, not an Instagram requirement.

Keep a practitioner's suggestion as a recommendation until relevant evidence warrants a default. Promote a repeated own-content finding with its sources, scope and limitations; keep contrary cases. Revise the skill only when the procedure changes. Keep research, underlying explanations and source history in docs so the agent can choose rather than recite a single template.
