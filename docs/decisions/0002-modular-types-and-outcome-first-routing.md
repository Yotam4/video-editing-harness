# Modular types and outcome-first routing

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: accepted scope from the owner's request; specific starter recipes and recommendations are initial proposals to refine through use.

## Decision

Make the content harness modular across generic social patterns and Ela Maayan's own types. Classify each new item by format, recipe, purpose, audience stage and intended outcome. Support unfamiliar types as candidates. Use an outcome-first brainstorm before new concepts and a lighter path for approved revisions.

Add a sourced social/Instagram knowledge base, own-content evidence, explainable registry/classifier, and thin routing, brainstorming and product-video procedures. Expand app demos, feature stories, verified proof videos and motion explainers alongside existing carousels and natural footage Reels.

## Constraints

- Git remains canonical; Notion is a mirror under the Ela Maayan Hub; media/raw exports stay external.
- No mandatory sales funnel, install pitch or CTA for every post.
- Separate measured evidence, platform fact, opinion and experiment. No guaranteed virality or fake retention metrics.
- Reuse actual Maayan brainstorming and verified licensed sources proportionately; software approval gates are not automatically social-production gates.
- Tal's exact video-harness identity must be verified before claiming an import. Any independent alternative must be attributed as a different source.
- A new topic or unfamiliar wording does not automatically become a permanent type or skill.

## Verification

Validate the registry, exercise ambiguous/unknown/entertainment classifications, forward-test the entry procedures with realistic briefs, and preserve actual results and unresolved source limitations. The system does not claim to have produced or measured a new creative artifact merely because its recipe exists.
