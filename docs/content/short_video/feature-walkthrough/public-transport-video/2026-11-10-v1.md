## Classification and brief

- Format/placement: short_video, 4:5 feed/carousel insert; 1080×1350, 30 fps, 10.8 seconds.
- Recipe: feature-walkthrough, matched against the existing registry; no new type or skill.
- Purpose: product_understanding and education; secondary existing-user activation.
- Audience: people planning an outing without a car; both evaluation and existing_user are planning hypotheses.
- Intended outcome: recognize the actual navigation/Moovit choice and where the stop/walking information lives.
- CTA: none in this insert; the surrounding carousel owns its install invitation.
- Asset mode: authentic live UI and original place photograph, with editorial motion/captions.
- Campaign: existing organic public-transport draft. Standalone asset; carousel V10 itself is unchanged.
- Owner requested a short appealing walkthrough, around ten seconds, produced autonomously and reviewed.

## Harness and source boundary

Used first-party product-video playbook, AGENTS, brand/voice knowledge and existing content record from Yotam4/video-editing-harness at commit 4e7d6c6aeafcfabeb272d1917387ceee7e404a7e. ## Creative argument

A recognizable place becomes an actionable outing: tap navigation, see the transit app choice, then see the last walking segment. Keep authentic labels and short Hebrew. Motion directs the viewer to the real targets rather than adding unrelated effects.

## Proof and product inspection

Live route: https://www.elamaayan.com/place/b2310692-46e9-41ac-897a-d192d6abf375

Verified on 2026-11-10: the public place page, its navigation action, the actual Waze/Google Maps/Off-Road/Moovit chooser, and the stop נעמי שמר/עוזי חיטמן with approximately 900 m / 11 minutes walking and source update 7.11. Clicked the actual Moovit option. Its external web tripplan page returned Moovit's own 403 page in this browser. Do not infer that the physical-phone app handoff fails, but do not claim a verified external itinerary. The film shows neither a fabricated route nor a successful external result.

The source screenshots are real pixels captured from the live app. This is an edited screen-state walkthrough with tap indicators, focus frames, caption reveals, a gentle push-in and transitions; it is not represented as a continuous live screen recording. The return to the place-page details is explicitly captioned so it is not confused with a Moovit result. The named-stop/walking crop is contextualized as the Ein Afek example, not universal coverage.
