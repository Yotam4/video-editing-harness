## Decisions to carry forward

- Use destination collections for practical saves/shares, and founder/community stories for connection and follows. Keep product value visible when announcing features.
- Preserve real people, actual places, and readable product evidence. These are observed creative conventions and owner preferences; their isolated engagement effect has not been tested.
- Offer questions, specific benefits, concrete scenes, and honest contrasts as alternative openings. Avoid swearing at the start by owner preference.
- Use the template skill for exact Drive lineage. Do not call every approved design successful, or assume a later export is the one published.
- Test one visual change at a time: subject/text separation, crop, headline position, or screenshot size. Measure the chosen goal and avoid turning every test into a new template.

## research-and-measurement.md

# Research and measurement

Checked 2026-11-10. Keep platform facts dated; recheck volatile claims before presenting them as current. Consult `self-attention` for the detailed carousel evidence ledger.

## Research conclusions

- Specific, relevant openings and a fulfilled promise are useful editorial principles. A question is one option, not a demonstrated universal winner. Buffer's [hook guidance](https://buffer.com/resources/good-hooks/) is practitioner advice, not a controlled trial on our audience.
- Mosseri's [Colin and Samir interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxizdQ2hRfk), published 2026-10-26, describes deeper carousel engagement beyond time spent. The [searchable third-party transcript](https://smashnotes.com/p/the-colin-and-samir-show/e/why-instagram-changed-its-mind-on-long-form-video), around 22:52–24:10, was read; the original video was not watched. Music eligibility and possible resurfacing at item 2 are opportunities, not guaranteed boosts.
- The [Meta Feed system card](https://ai.meta.com/tools/system-cards/instagram-feed-ranking/) describes predicted interest/actions; its 2022 update is general context, not evidence of today's exact algorithm weights.
- [Social Media Today's 2025 reporting](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) attributes watch time, likes/reach, and sends/reach guidance to Mosseri. Treat this as attributed reporting; current creator-site retrieval was rate-limited. Do not relabel our shares metric as DM sends.
- No verified evidence in this research establishes a blanket Instagram penalty for a swear word in the opening. Keep the no-swear opening as the owner's preference. Avoid fake prohibited-word lists.
- Broad Buffer/Socialinsider format benchmarks can inspire questions, but our source snapshot controls claims about Ela Maayan. Keep different denominators and study periods separate.

## Snapshot schema

One row per publication; `date_key` preserves Metricool's returned timestamp string. `format` is image/carousel/reel. `theme` is descriptive caption coding, not a measured cause. `reach`, `views`, `likes`, `comments`, `saves`, `shares`, and feed-post `follows` are returned metrics. Blank means unavailable. Organic fields were requested; blank paid fields do not verify no boost. `watch_seconds`, `view_rate_3s_pct`, and `skip_rate_3s_pct` apply only to Reels. Do not compute a missing Reel follow count as zero.

Formulae: saves per 100 reached = 100<i>saves/reach; shares per 100 reached = 100</i>shares/reach; follows per 100 reached = 100\*follows/reach when present. Report the median of per-post rates separately from aggregate sums divided by summed reach. Never sum publication reaches and call the result unique audience.

## Practical tests

For the next several comparable posts, choose one variable and one main metric. For example, test a closer screenshot crop for feature understanding, a stronger second carousel hook for interest, or a clearer first visual action for early Reel attention. Record other changes rather than hiding confounders.

Capture results at matched ages such as 24h and 7d when available. Recommend this collection plan without scheduling it unless asked. Review shares/reach, saves/reach, feed follows/reach, and available Reel view/skip rate plus watch time. Use tracked visits/installs for conversion only if instrumentation and attribution exist.

Do not claim measured slide drop-off or completion without those fields. Do not derive text placement effects from captions. Link each visual observation to the actual asset inspected and mark exact matches, candidates, drafts, and missing creative separately.
