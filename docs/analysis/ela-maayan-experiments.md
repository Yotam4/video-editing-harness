# Ela Maayan creative experiments

Prepared 2026-11-10 from the [refreshed own-content baseline](ela-maayan-content-evidence-2026-11-10.md). These are recommended tests, not promises of reach, installed automations, or new permanent content types.

## Start with the viewer's job

Choose an intended outcome before choosing a funnel label: enjoyment, recognition, useful planning, understanding, participation, trust or a concrete product action. Define the viewer's starting knowledge and what should change. A CTA can be absent when the piece already delivers its intended value.

Select one primary outcome and a small number of guardrails. Use the closest own-account examples as a baseline, not generic industry averages. Record the type, audience state, distribution, collaborator/promotion status, hypothesis, changed variable, source asset, exact approved copy, output version and measurement window in the Git content record.

The product-demo, proof-video and animation items are especially useful to prototype because the current review does not establish a validated own-account cohort for them. This is an opportunity to learn, not evidence that they will outperform existing carousels.

## Measurement protocol

1. Write the hypothesis and primary outcome before producing the variants. Keep one meaningful variable different when feasible; log all other differences instead of hiding them.
2. Prefer a small private comprehension review before publishing an instructional asset. Ask what the product lets the viewer do and what they would do next; do not tell them the intended answer first.
3. Collect available publication metrics at consistent ages, for example 24 hours and seven days. This is a collection plan, not a scheduled task. Use exact extraction timestamps and record fields absent from the provider.
4. Compare within format and a close topic/audience/distribution context. Match or annotate publication age, campaign, collaborator, boost/trial state and seasonal context. Treat sequential organic posts as observational evidence, not randomized A/B tests.
5. Calculate per-publication saves/reach, shares/reach and feed follows/reach when available. Keep views, reach and followers as separate denominators. Do not relabel shares as DM sends or unavailable Reel follows as zero.
6. For Reel attention, use the exposed three-second view/skip rate and watch time. The current connector results lacked duration and retention; do not calculate completion from a guessed duration. No carousel slide-drop-off metric was available.
7. Judge a conversion objective using attributed clicks, feature starts, activations or installs only when the instrumentation exists and attribution limits are stated. A post with few shares may still have served a narrow audience; the present data cannot establish that it failed to convert.
8. Capture accurate qualitative feedback for connection, story, humor or brand recognition. Do not manufacture a numeric proxy just to force those jobs into a sales funnel.

## Decision after a test

- **Keep as a variant:** it fits the creative job, but evidence is too small, mixed or confounded to promote.
- **Repeat deliberately:** a promising observation deserves another close comparison.
- **Promote a reusable default:** several relevant examples support it, production constraints are understood, and the rule's scope and limitations are documented.
- **Retire or revise:** the promised value was unclear, facts/UI were wrong, or a repeated relevant comparison supports a change. Preserve the old rationale and version.

Do not create one skill per campaign or automatically promote a new type from one unusual brief. A reusable subtype needs a recurring job, different required inputs or production steps, a distinct quality checklist and at least a reviewable example. A temporary candidate can use generic production modules while evidence accumulates.

## Suggested first three prototypes

These are recommendations, not tasks already produced or published:

1. **One-feature screen demo:** show a genuine viewer task, one action and the result, using a current screen recording. Start with product understanding; add a download/use CTA only if that is the intended next step.
2. **Proof-led progress clip:** one verified useful capability or a dated, sourced milestone, with actual product evidence. Keep the claim narrower than the proof.
3. **Existing place carousel with a stronger slide 2:** keep the successful destination-card family and factual utility while testing a standalone second entry. This changes less than launching an entirely new style.
