## Definitions and limits

- Rate = `100 × metric / publication reach`. The tables report percentages, not Metricool's separately labeled engagement statistic. Group statistics are medians of individual post rates, not pooled rates.
- Reach is publication-level audience; adding it across posts does not produce unique account audience. Views can include repeat exposure.
- `shares` is the connector's shares field, not verified DM sends. Feed follows are publication-attributed; Reel follows were unavailable and are not zero.
- Themes below were manually coded from captions. Captions do not reveal spoken hooks, first-frame text, editing or actual on-screen behavior. Visual inspection is described separately.
- Lifetime snapshots have unequal publication ages. Audience size, season, topic, collaboration, promotion, CTA and distribution differ. Even a same-format comparison is not an isolated treatment effect.
- Two Reels with reach 10 and 20 are retained in the population count but excluded from the practical `reach >= 100` descriptive baseline. Check trial/draft/collaboration/availability state before interpreting them. The threshold is a working filter, not an Instagram rule.
- No slide-by-slide completion, dwell, drop-off, attributed installs or activation metrics were returned. Intended funnel role is editorial classification, not measured customer movement.

## Modular routing recommendations

| Intended job | Useful starting module | Evidence status | What to preserve/test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Save a practical next trip | Destination/route collection | Repeated observational support | Real destinations, verified access, early useful detail; compare save/share rates within similar collections. |
| Know the people and reason behind us | Founder/community story | One breakout plus ordinary outcomes | Specific true moments and voice; connection is valid without a sales ending. |
| Understand one app behavior | Feature walkthrough or screen demo | Production opportunity, not an own-account proven winner | A real screen, one recognizable viewer task, visible outcome; test comprehension before aesthetic polish. |
| See credible product progress | Proof/brag video | New experiment; no validated demo cohort in this review | Date and source every claim; show product evidence, not unsupported superlatives. |
| Enjoy a natural moment with friends | Natural Reel/field story | Owner-approved mode with mixed observed results | Preserve motion, framing and voice; choose enjoyment/connection as legitimate outcomes. |
| Decide to install/use a feature | Benefit-led demo or commercial | Conversion attribution missing | Instrument a relevant next step; do not judge only on shares or claim installations from comments. |

Funnel labels describe the viewer's starting context and intended movement. A practical carousel can discover, educate and activate; a Reel is not automatically top-of-funnel, and product content is not automatically bottom-of-funnel. Use the registry's separate format, subtype, audience state, outcome and CTA fields.

## What can become a default

**Owner preferences:** natural Israeli Hebrew; preserve approved copy; avoid swearing at the opening; use real people/places/screens; clean phone-readable layouts. These are not algorithm penalties or experimentally isolated growth laws.

**Observed patterns:** multiple geographic collections earn useful saves/shares; one founder story was a follower breakout; related Gush Etzion Reels exceeded the descriptive baseline; approved designs also have ordinary outcomes.

**Experiments:** clearer benefit-first product demos, larger readable UI crops, independently meaningful second carousel slides, varied opening structures, authentic proof videos. See [the experiment backlog](ela-maayan-experiments.md). They must remain recommendations until measured evidence supports promotion.

Revisit after several comparable new publications, a changed audience/product objective, a verified publication-to-asset join, or an instrumentation change. Do not schedule a refresh merely because this document exists.
