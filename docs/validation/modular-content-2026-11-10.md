Checked: 2026-11-10. Scope: the content system, classification helper and thin procedures. This is not a test of Instagram performance or a new video renderer.

## Research and source boundaries

Five parallel research/implementation workstreams covered Instagram/social evidence, type/funnel taxonomy, our own content, video workflows and brainstorming. The Instagram ledger records 25 sources and 18 claims with access status and limitations. Current inaccessible originals remain unresolved rather than becoming newly asserted rules.

Own-content work verified the connected brand and refreshed 29 feed publications and 27 Reels; the analysis preserves post-age, paid-status, creative-match and attribution limits. Brag and SaaS demo sources were inspected at pinned revisions as separate references. Tal's exact harness URL remains unidentified; no source was relabeled or silently imported. No third-party renderer, example media or music was installed.

## Independent brief exercises

Fresh agents used the actual personal procedures and checkout, without rendering or changing operational records:

| Brief | Observed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Approved transport carousel, highlight Moovit only | Preserved words/forum and inherited the existing recipe; no new brainstorm |
| Friends arguing about a forgotten towel, no pitch | Entertainment outcome, no CTA or required funnel; real footage review remained necessary |
| Explain forum using screenshots, no recording | Proposed screenshot-led carousel with real context; did not invent operational taps |
| Branching choose-your-own-trip story | Proposed a candidate and a navigation prototype; did not register a permanent skill |
| Hot-springs feature/proof video with uncertain release and unsupported usage claim | Withheld the claim, separated draft-design preview from a released-feature demo, and proposed proof/capture requirements and scene acceptance checks |

The raw helper initially matched a negated “screen recording” phrase. The implementation now discloses excluded negated signals. Approved-revision mode accepts a stored recipe; without one it requests the existing record rather than inventing a recipe. Targeted reruns confirmed these outcomes.

## Practical limits

The classifier is lexical assistance, not semantic AI or a prediction of reach. Human/conversational judgment remains necessary. Starter recipes are not all established by Ela Maayan production results. Personal-skill discovery is contextual, and old chats may retain cached catalogs; the repository entry point or explicit invocation remains available.

No video was rendered, production result measured, post published, schedule created or automatic knowledge-sync service installed in this work. Media/raw exports remain outside Git. Knowledge is canonical here and mirrored only under the Ela Maayan Hub.
