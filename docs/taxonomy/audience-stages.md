# Audience stages, purpose and callouts

Checked 2026-11-10. **The table below is our planning synthesis**, not an Instagram rule, guaranteed conversion model or a diagnosis of each viewer. A funnel is one way to describe a campaign’s intended role. Not every piece has a sales role, and viewers do not reliably pass through posts in a fixed order.

## What the underlying research supports

Google’s original consumer research describes exploration and evaluation as recurring modes rather than a single straight purchase path. Its 2020 study combines literature review, observation, search analysis and simulated shopping experiments. The useful implication here is to give people relevant context and evidence wherever they encounter a piece. Its purchase findings are not measured evidence about Ela Maayan’s Instagram, app installs or organic carousel performance. Source: [Rennie and Protheroe, July 2020](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/consumer-insights/navigating-purchase-behavior-and-decision-making/).

LinkedIn’s B2B Institute presents the Ehrenberg-Bass “95-5” argument to distinguish present demand from future brand demand. Its underlying examples concern category buying cycles, especially B2B. We use it as a challenge to requiring every post to convert immediately; **we do not apply 95% or 5% as measured proportions of our audience** or a posting allocation. Source: [LinkedIn B2B Institute, 95-5 Rule](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/resources/b2b-institute/b2b-research/trends/95-5-rule), accessed 2026-11-10; page publication date not stated in the retrieved page.

These sources justify keeping audience context and intended outcome explicit. The exact creative treatments and measurement choices below are recommendations to test, informed also by our current brand policy allowing story, humor, beauty and friendship without a CTA.

## Choose a role for this piece

| Audience context | Brief question | Emphasize | Useful proof/callout | CTA, when appropriate | Evidence to observe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery / TOFU | Why would someone unfamiliar care now? | Recognizable situation, feeling, useful promise or human moment; enough identity to be understood | Real scene, concrete example, distinctive people/place; simple introduction if needed | Often none; share/save/profile visit only when it follows the value | Qualified reach, non-follower reach when available, shares/reach, subsequent interest with attribution limits |
| Evaluation / MOFU | What are they trying to understand or compare? | Specific usefulness, fit, limits, a fair comparison or actual workflow | Genuine screenshots, actual steps, current conditions, transparent criteria | Try an example, learn more, save details, ask a relevant question | Saves/reach, useful questions, tracked detail views or feature interest when available |
| Action / BOFU | What specific action are they ready and able to take? | Exact next step, effort, availability and conditions | Working destination, real current interface, verified terms or demonstration | One clear action such as open feature, install, RSVP or contact; none if action is not the piece’s job | Action completion and downstream quality, with actual event definitions and attribution limits |
| Existing user / retention | What would help someone already using us? | A useful action, newly available capability, correction or a reason to return | Current app path and visible success state; honest eligibility/limitations | Open relevant feature or apply the tip; no need to pitch installation to existing users | Feature use, return use and task completion when measurable |
| Community / relationship | What will strengthen recognition, belonging or conversation? | Specific people, contribution, humor and real experience | Approved story, authentic footage or contribution | None, genuine reply or participation if invited by the content | Meaningful responses, repeat participation, qualitative relevance; do not count every comment as trust |
| Not applicable | Is the point simply enjoyment, a story, beauty or expression? | The experience and its payoff | Honest scene or clear fictional treatment | None is a complete choice | Audience response appropriate to the creative aim; avoid inventing a conversion expectation |
| Unknown | What assumption would materially change the concept? | State uncertainty before selecting the treatment | Ask one useful question or compare two concepts | Leave undecided | Decide what evidence would make this experiment informative |

`community` is a purpose; the registry uses `existing_user`, `discovery` or `not_applicable` as the corresponding audience-stage label depending on the actual brief. Do not add a new stage just because the post is a community story.

## The same app feature, three treatments

| Treatment | Opening | Evidence highlighted | Payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | A recognizable no-car hiking situation | One real destination and a visible transport option | A useful possibility, with optional invitation to explore |
| Evaluation | How does public transport navigation work here? | Actual current screen, supported navigation choices, limitations | Viewer understands the workflow and whether it fits |
| Existing user/action | Where to tap for public transport directions | Exact current entry point and resulting navigation action | Viewer can perform the task |

These are concept directions, not verified feature copy. Check current app behavior before naming controls or making a navigation claim. A founder story about why the feature matters may instead serve connection, without needing to become any of these treatments.

## Callouts follow useful information

- Discovery: identify the situation and promise; a big product badge is not automatically the hook.
- Evaluation: make the important evidence readable. A screenshot cannot prove behavior when its relevant control is hidden or too small.
- Action: show the real next step and conditions. Do not invent urgency, scarcity, discounts, social proof or frictionless onboarding.
- Entertainment/connection: protect the joke, human moment or image. An appended download pitch can weaken the intended experience; test rather than enforce it.
- Across stages: preserve approved Hebrew, RTL, source provenance and accessible contrast. Changes in stage do not override truth or permissions.

## Measurement cautions

Choose the primary intended outcome before comparing pieces. Report the metric’s numerator, denominator, source and observation window; available watch time does not necessarily reveal completion, and aggregate carousel data does not reveal slide-by-slide retention. Compare like formats, topics and post ages where possible. Separate organic, paid and partner distributions when known; missing paid fields mean unknown.

A single successful piece does not establish that its funnel label, hook or CTA caused the result. Maintain a testable recommendation and preserve mixed findings. A brand connection piece can be successful on its own terms without proving that it produced installs.
